MuziiNap is an Android music player and streaming app built with Flutter.

Features:
- Search and play music through supported YouTube and YouTube Music integrations
- Background playback with notification and media controls
- Playback queue management and playback restoration
- Song caching and downloads, with offline playback when files are available locally
- Playlist creation, favourites, bookmarks, and album/artist library views
- Import supported song, playlist, album, and artist links through Android sharing
- Share and copy canonical MuziiNap song links; generate song QR content
- Streaming quality controls and configured stream-source fallback
- Custom equalizer and Android audio integration
- Synced and plain lyrics, lyrics reload, and live lyrics translation
- Radio, sleep timer, skip silence, and dynamic artwork-based theming
- Android Auto support
- QR-code generation, sharing, and scanning
- Configurable navigation, player gestures, and playback settings
- Multiple language/localization support

Core app use does not require a MuziiNap account. YouTube sign-in may be used for personalized or account-dependent features.

Feature availability depends on Android version, device audio support, network conditions, and third-party providers. Stream availability and third-party service behavior can change independently of the app.
